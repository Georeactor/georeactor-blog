## Asides

Cryptocurrency reporter Molly White [noticed](https://bsky.app/profile/molly.wiki/post/3mnmqc32re22p) that NYT Cooking has fewer meat recipes recently. More "plain spaghetti but a little spicy". <br/>
If you want a more direct recession indicator, a YouTuber posted a video [Beef Is Too Expensive, So I Make This Ragu Instead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-zr0Wj2w9-E) with chicken thighs.

I've just learned about Dutch Crunch, a Bay Area bread: https://www.sfgate.com/food/article/dutch-crunch-bread-17635355.php

I also learned that Thai restaurants in Chicago will make a "crispy" pad see ew: https://buffetmagazine.net/chicago-thai-food-pad-see-ew/

I got a two-piece petite steak from Target (??) and it was the right price and easy to cook (??).

Someone was talking up [beau monde seasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beau_Monde_seasoning). There is one brand making this, and it is good on a few foods. Meanwhile I bought Lime Pickle but have yet to apply it, maybe on a tuna melt?

While looking up Indonesian noodles, there is a chain [WizzMie](https://www.instagram.com/wizzmie/) which has fewer locations than the one I know (Mie Gacoan), but they also have sushi and other Japanese foods.

*Ravenous* had a mini-article about the Pope receiving a jar of giardiniera from a Chicago business.

I tried a highly-rated pizza place a little further outside my usual neighborhood bubble, and it was bad! When I was a kid I never ate pizza, like I would get breadsticks when the family got it, so I know that I have weird preferences. These days I will typically pick something with a few toppings or some kind of fusion (i.e. artichoke, beet pesto, Indian) over another pepperoni. My global favorite is Nello's in Phoenix, with an unhealthy dollop of ricotta cheese on each slice, after taking a desert hike. Anyway I tried this  place with sun-dried tomato, a drizzle of pesto, and balsamic glaze, and it did not work for me.

### One-Pot Orzo and Meatballs

https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/773675806-one-pot-orzo-and-meatballs

This is an unusual recipe (one-ingredient meatballs? toasting orzo, then tomato paste, then water?) but it worked well and fit in one pot. The other day I made two burritos and then I was able to use the remaining chorizo here. I shredded one big basil leaf at the end. I didn't feel that it needed  any cheese.

There is a lot of variation in orzo, and Whole Foods has is my gold standard. I bought a thing of RiceSelect tri-color orzo about a year ago and it wasn't worth it.
