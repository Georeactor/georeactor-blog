### History

If the Greeks knew the shape and size of the Earth, the distance to the moon, and so on, why did they never make a globe / solar system model? <br/>
It looks like globes only took off in the 1400s, with the oldest existing one from 1490–1492 just in time to be missing the Americas. There is a [2000-year-old Roman sculpture of Atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farnese_Atlas) with a 'celestial sphere' of the night sky, but not a map of the ground.

### Pop Culture

Sarah Wynn-Williams's Facebook exposé *Careless People* opens with a shark attack. Locals in New Zealand [have long believed](https://www.stuff.co.nz/nz-news/360618281/mystery-shark-attack-victim-revealed-sarah-wynn-williams) it was a stingray. Supporters point out that she has at least been consistent with the story, and told it anonymously on *This American Life* [in 2012](https://www.thisamericanlife.org/476/what-doesnt-kill-you/act-two-2).

Did Matt Damon invent the now-popular idea that Netflix expects creators to "explain the plot four or five times" for distracted viewers, or was he himself bamboozled? As best I can tell this first floated into public discourse in [a PC Mag article](https://www.pcmag.com/news/netflix-is-telling-writers-to-dumb-down-shows-since-viewers-are-on-their) in January 2025; it was not so explicit in a linked story [from n+1 Magazine](https://www.nplusonemag.com/issue-49/essays/casual-viewing/). Then Damon discusses it in a Joe Rogan interview in January 2026. I listen to a screenwriting podcast which has (I think?) referenced this a couple of times without critiquing it. It sounds like the sort of complaint you would hear from Damon and (a) not put on the record that it's from a famous actor-director, and (b) not doubt it because he knows the industry, right? <br/>
After a bit at the Oscars, [Netflix denied it](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/netflix-execs-not-asking-repeat-tv-and-film-plots-1236692745/).

In 2016 the *New York Times* had a longform article about the life of a garment / sweatshop worker in India. A few details of their village culture before migrating to the city were so extreme, people called it out on Hacker News. As far as I know no one identified the culture specifically, so maybe it's a true micro-culture and 'the internet' does not know about it?

Does the decline of Twitter (even pre-Elon) give us any insights into how a longlived platform like YouTube might fail someday?

Does the *Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* have potential to remain a pop culture fixture, or is it too British? My brother and I got into it just in time for the 2005 movie, but the characters, overall concept, and recognizable jokes (such as "Mostly Harmless") are rarely referenced.

I wonder what it'd be like to re-read *October Sky* now that I'm an adult? There was a lot going on. Hickam has written more fiction and memoirs. When he posts about politics he is a MAGA guy, which makes sense for a boomer from West Virginia, but I can't follow his Facebook anymore.

### Weird to think about

Should we be more worried about parasites? Kristen Bell did a talk show circuit in 2018 where she was open about [pinworms](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eukcR2nMYww). I have traveled a lot and got worried enough to try Combantrin antiparasitic chocolate, but didn't really learn anything.

Our elementary school librarian read to us from *A Child Called It* about child abuse, *They Cage The Animals At Night* about a group home, and *Ella Enchanted*. Maybe this came with good intentions or she was processing things? But this was only a few story times, so we never got any kind of resolution.

In middle school, we saw an anti-drug anthology series where some kids were in a vague grim purgatory afterlife? While doing the write-up I remembered we also saw the Ryan White TV movie, and with that timepoint found: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Nightmare_on_Drug_Street


### Super niche

In Romania, I talked myself into ordering a water instead of a Coke, but it was still in my mindspace. The waiter brought me a Coke and a minute later  apologized.

Me and my Airbnb host in Bilbao went to Biarritz on the same day without mentioning it or running into each other. I had a burning anxiety about figuring out the return bus, and she and her family actually ended up having problems with their return.

We had an exchange student who had to dump a sweater in the river because it of a curse from a relative? I wasn't there for this.
